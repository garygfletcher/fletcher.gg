MARITIME HERITAGE

Keeping history afloat.

I skipper Gerfalcon, a Dunkirk Little Ship and former Royal Navy patrol vessel. Her restoration brings together practical engineering, historical research and a commitment to preserving Britain’s maritime heritage.

The restoration received two volunteer awards and was shortlisted for the Martyn Heighton Award for maritime heritage conservation. In 2025, Gerfalcon returned to Dunkirk for the evacuation’s 85th anniversary.

COACHING & EXPEDITIONS

Life on the water.

From a solo English Channel crossing to Arctic expeditions and the Great Bahama Bank, paddlesport has taken me well beyond familiar waters.

On 3 October 2022, I crossed from Dungeness to Boulogne-sur-Mer in 7 hours, 21 minutes and 43 seconds. Performance Sea Kayak lists my crossing as solo and RIB-assisted, with a RIB transfer across the French shipping lane.

Through kayak.coach and Windsor Canoe Club, I coach paddlers and support adventurous expeditions. I previously coached at Battersea Canoe Club and Poole Harbour Canoe Club. As a Paddle UK committee member, I also contribute to the sport’s governance and community development.
